# DockVision

At DockVision, we empower researchers, universities, and biotech teams with end-to-end bioinformatics and computational biology solutions. Our expertise spans structural bioinformatics; genomics, transcriptomics and sequence analysis; scientific programming and statistical data analysis; and applied AI and machine learning in healthcare.

## What we do

- Genomics, Transcriptomics & Proteomics
- Structural Bioinformatics
- Machine Learning & AI in Healthcare and Life Sciences
- Data Science (Python & R)

## Tools we use

- **Molecular docking:** AutoDock Vina, AutoDock Tools (MGLTools), HADDOCK, DiffDock
- **Molecular dynamics:** GROMACS, gmx_MMPBSA (MM-PBSA / MM-GBSA), CHARMM-GUI
- **Structure & visualisation:** AlphaFold, PyMOL, UCSF Chimera, Discovery Studio Visualizer, Avogadro, Open Babel, DoGSite3
- **Cheminformatics & ADMET:** RDKit, SwissADME, pkCSM, CoMSIA
- **Genomics & transcriptomics:** FastQC, MultiQC, fastp, HISAT2, STAR, Salmon, featureCounts, DESeq2, edgeR, limma, clusterProfiler, GSEA
- **Variant analysis:** BWA, SAMtools, bcftools, GATK
- **Single-cell:** Seurat v5, Scanpy, Harmony, Monocle3, CellChat
- **Machine learning & AI:** scikit-learn, XGBoost, TensorFlow/Keras, PyTorch, Optuna, SHAP, Captum
- **Data science:** Python (pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, seaborn), R (tidyverse, ggplot2, Bioconductor), Jupyter, RStudio, Linux/Bash

## Featured work

- **[dockvision-case-studies](https://github.com/Dock-Vision/dockvision-case-studies)** — the 27 case studies published on dockvisionbio.com, each with its figure and a short write-up.
- **[docking-md-demo](https://github.com/Dock-Vision/docking-md-demo)** — runnable docking and MD workflow on public data: EGFR kinase (PDB 1M17) with erlotinib, AutoDock Vina redocking (top pose 1.67 Å from the crystal pose) and a short GROMACS simulation.
- **[rnaseq-demo](https://github.com/Dock-Vision/rnaseq-demo)** — reproducible bulk RNA-seq analysis in R on the public airway dataset: DESeq2, PCA, volcano plot, heatmap and GO/KEGG enrichment.
